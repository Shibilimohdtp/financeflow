Stillwater

Calm clarity over your own money — private by default, precise where numbers matter, warm where people matter, and intelligent only when asked.

Product personality: The Quiet Steward — a discreet, competent friend who keeps immaculate personal records, never gossips about your spending, explains things plainly, and speaks up only when it genuinely helps.

Privacy-first · Local-first · Works 100% offline Material 3 compatible · Flutter · Android-first Version 1.0 AI on a leash — optional, explainable, undoable
02 · Foundation

Design principles

Six commitments that settle every design argument in FinanceFlow. Water responds — it never performs.

Calm financial clarity

Clarity over decoration. Every pixel earns its place with information or orientation. Nothing flashes, shouts, or pressures.

Privacy by default

On-device status is shown, not hidden. Anything that may leave the device crosses the amber Boundary — always with consent.

Personal control

Every suggestion is undoable, every source is visible, every deletion is explicit. The user is the only authority.

Intelligent assistance

AI appears as small warm sparks — labelled, confidence-rated, explainable. A quiet assistant, never a chatbot mascot.

Progress without judgment

Overspending is reported as information, never as failure. Warm clay, not alarm red. No shame language, ever.

Offline resilience

Offline is the hero state, not an error. “Offline — everything still works.” The full app functions with zero connectivity.

Semantic colours — information, not alarm

Negative financial states use warm clay, never alarm red. True red-orange is reserved for system errors and destructive confirmation. Colour is never the only signal — sign glyphs and icons always accompany it.

Financial semantics

Avoid: red for overspending · marigold text on white · green/clay adjacent without labels · pure black or pure white large surfaces.

13 · Trust

Privacy & security

Two visual languages: on-device calm (shield-check, green on soft teal) and the Boundary (dashed amber) for anything that may leave the device. Nothing external is ever pre-checked. Reassurance, never fear.

Local-only data

Transactions: 12,431 · stored on this phone · 0 on servers. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Encrypted on this device

Database collections are encrypted with a keystore-backed key. Backups are encrypted before they’re written.

Offline — everything still works

FinanceFlow has no server to lose. Being offline is the purest form of private.

FinanceFlow is locked

Touch the sensor or enter your PIN. Balances stay hidden until you unlock — even in app previews.

Screenshot protection on
Permission request — SMS access

FinanceFlow reads transaction SMS only, on this device, to capture payments from your bank.

✓ Reads: debit/credit alerts from banks you choose ✗ Never reads: OTPs, personal messages, contacts ✓ You can switch this off anytime — manual entry and CSV still work
Leaves this device
Gemini assistance

When enabled, uncategorised merchant names and amounts are sent to Gemini. Full statements are never sent. Optional — offline rules keep working.

Your storage, your choice Encrypted backup destination

The backup is an encrypted ZIP only your passphrase can open. Nothing touches a FinanceFlow server — there isn’t one.

Delete all data?

This erases 12,431 transactions, budgets and goals from this phone only. Backups you exported are not affected. This cannot be undone.

18 · Voice

UX writing examples

The Quiet Steward speaks: plain, calm, owning, honest about limits. Facts + scope + option — never blame, never fear, never exclamation marks on money matters.

Empty state
“Nothing here yet — and that’s fine. Transactions appear the moment a source captures them.”
Warm Fraunces headline + the fastest next step. Never “You haven’t added anything!”
Budget warning
“Transport is ₹390 from its limit. This is mostly from three fuel payments this week. You can adjust the budget or review these payments.”
Fact + scope + option. No “You overspent!”, no red banners.
AI suggestion
“Suggested: Groceries · high match. Because: ‘Swiggy Instamart’ matched your saved rule. Ran on-device.”
Confidence, reason, and where it ran — in one breath.
Duplicate
“Looks like the same payment captured twice (notification + SMS). Merge them, or keep both?”
The user decides; merges are undoable for 30 days.
Privacy disclosure
“When enabled, merchant names and amounts go to Gemini — never full statements. Everything else stays on this phone.”
Boundary formula: what goes where, only when.
Offline mode
“Offline — everything still works.”
Offline is a feature, not an error. No degraded styling.
Error
“Couldn’t read ‘march.pdf’. It may not be a statement file. Your other imports are safe.”
What happened + reassurance + next step. No stack traces up front.
Success
“Saved on this device.”
Every success quietly re-states the privacy promise.
Backup
“Encrypted backup saved to your Drive folder. Only your passphrase can open it.”
Destination in plain words; ownership stays with the user.
Restore
“Restored. Everything is encrypted on this device again.” Wrong passphrase: “That passphrase didn’t match. Try again.”
Neutral on failure — no lockout drama, no accusation.
